## Image quality

Original documentary photos remain native-resolution lossless WebP. The browser now selects sufficiently large responsive files and the gallery stage/lightbox uses the native original. Tiny generated promotional-card crops have been replaced with original photographs. Source photos and old article clippings still have their original resolution limits; no artificial face reconstruction or invented detail is claimed.

## New paper asset

Built-in ImageGen, exact underlying model not selectable. Saved asset: `public/assets/newspaper-paper.webp`.

Prompt: “High-resolution flat seamless pale antique ivory newspaper paper, warm pale cream #f3e8d0, subtle tan foxing, fibrous pulp, tiny scattered fibers, soft age mottling and gentle wrinkles. Bright and readable. Orthographic scan, entire canvas paper. No text, letters, icons, objects, borders, burn marks or heavy coffee stains. Continuous non-directional texture for a CSS background underneath navy type.”

The final browser presentation adds an ivory overlay to match the approved paper's brightness.
